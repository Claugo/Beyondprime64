Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

1. Data controller

This website, beyondprime64.org, is managed by Claudio Govi, independent researcher. For any privacy-related enquiry, use the contact details in section 7.

2. What data we collect

This site does not collect personal data directly. The only data that may be processed relates to site usage analytics:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Browser type and operating system (aggregated, not personally identifiable)
  • Approximate geographic region (country level only)
  • Referring URL (where you came from)

No names, email addresses, or personally identifiable information are collected or stored. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

3. Cookies

This site may use minimal analytics cookies to understand how visitors interact with the content. These cookies do not track individuals across websites and do not contain personally identifiable information.

When you first visit the site, you are asked to accept or decline these cookies. If you decline, no analytics cookies are set. You can change your preference at any time using the "Cookie settings" link in the site footer.

4. Legal basis for processing

Analytics data is processed on the basis of legitimate interest (understanding site usage to improve content) and, where required, consent (via the cookie banner). No processing occurs without at least one valid legal basis under GDPR Article 6.

5. Data retention

Aggregated analytics data is retained for a maximum of 26 months, after which it is automatically deleted. No individual-level data is retained beyond a single session unless a cookie is explicitly accepted.

6. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access any personal data held about you
  • Rectification of inaccurate data
  • Erasure of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restriction of processing
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Data portability where technically feasible

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond within 30 days.
